Expose an Icon({ library: "lucide" }) contract helper for Zod, Valibot and ArkType, with a matching React renderer. Load validated icon geometry on demand from a versioned catalogue, with request deduplication, caching, stable dimensions and accessible labels. Hosts can configure a self-hosted catalogue URL or fetch implementation.
